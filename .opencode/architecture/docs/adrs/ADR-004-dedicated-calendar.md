# ADR-004 - Dedicated Calendar

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Generated events must not mix visually and logically with personal events.

---

## Decision

Create a dedicated calendar.

Name:

BrkRoutnXdle

---

## Responsibilities

### Primary Calendar

Read Only.

### BrkRoutnXdle Calendar

Read + Write.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

* Clear separation.
* Lower risk of conflict.
* Recalculation becomes safer.

### Negative

* User will have one more calendar.
